Gmail

  1. Click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new.
  3. Paste into the signature box (Cmd+V on Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows).
  4. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

Apple Mail (Mac)

  1. Open Mail, then Settings → Signatures.
  2. Click +, then paste into the right-hand box.
  3. Untick "Always match my default message font" so the colors stay.

Outlook

  1. Go to Settings → Mail → Compose and reply.
  2. Paste into the signature box and click Save.
